About

Hi, I'm Kyle, a UX and UI designer with a Master's in Design + Innovation and a graphic design undergraduate degree at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, where I focus on user experience, digital interfaces, and design strategy.

My work combines research, creativity, and problem-solving to design thoughtful experiences that help people navigate complex systems. I'm particularly interested in how design can simplify everyday interactions while creating meaningful stories.

How I Work

My work process.

01

Research

Stakeholder interviews, user interviews, observations, and affinity-diagram synthesis help me understand the real constraint before starting a project.

02

Define

Turning research into a clear problem statement, priorities, and success metrics everyone agrees on.

03

Design

Creating design assets, wireframes, and high-fidelity prototypes, then testing them with users before taking the product further.

04

Deliver

Working closely with partners and double-checking everything before handing off the project.
