Imagin8V Park — making the housing crisis visible

An interactive platform born from stakeholder conversations in my grad program, turning the unseen stories behind community-led housing and civic action into an explorable game.

Role

UX Researcher & Product Designer

Context

Graduate Capstone, January 2026–May 2026

Focus

Research Product Game Design
Overview

Making unseen stories visible.

Imagin8V Park is a project that grew out of conversations with our stakeholders and grad program about the housing crisis in Wisconsin.

We realized that the stories behind community-led housing and civic action are often unseen. Imagin8V Park aims to make these stories visible via an interactive platform — highlighting how education, participation, and shared spaces shape stronger, more stable communities amid an ongoing housing crisis.

Context

Grounded in real relationships.

Community trust doesn't come from a single meeting — it's built through iteration. Over the course of this project, we ran repeated feedback loops with residents, refining our approach each time we learned something new.

That groundwork became Adams Garden Park, a space shaped as much by the people who'd use it as by the people who designed it.

Phase 01

Research & discovery.

In the first phase of our project, our team conducted extensive research to better understand the potential impact of the game, identify user needs, and generate ideas through collaborative brainstorming sessions.

Phase 02

Building the world.

Phase 2 focused on the creation and development of the game. During this stage, our team explored game mechanics, developed mood boards, refined the narrative, designed characters, and began building interactive gameplay experiences.

Prototype

Low-fidelity prototype.

Approaching the end of Phase 2, core design elements were established, the narrative was fully developed, and a low-fidelity prototype of the game was created — setting up the next step: a high-fidelity prototype.

Refinement

High-fidelity design.

After completing the low-fidelity prototype, our team transitioned into full design production, shifting focus from structure and functionality to visual refinement and user experience. We iterated on layout, typography, color systems, and interactive elements to ensure the interface was both aesthetically cohesive and intuitive.

Throughout this phase, we incorporated feedback, resolved usability issues, and paid close attention to detail. The result: a high-fidelity prototype that accurately represents the intended user experience and is fully prepared for usability testing.

Results

Final results & testing.

After completing Stage 1 of the game, our team showcased our demo at StartingBlock Madison — presenting our work in a real-world setting and engaging directly with a diverse audience.

Testing with a live audience gave us deeper insight into user behavior, engagement, and overall experience, which is now shaping the next iteration of the game.
